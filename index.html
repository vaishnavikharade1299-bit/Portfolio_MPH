STI surveillance data pipeline and Tableau dashboard

Excel · Tableau

A county health department's line lists for syphilis, gonorrhea, chlamydia and HIV/AIDS came in as separate files per disease and year. I combined them into one analysis-ready dataset, documented every change, and built a descriptive-epidemiology dashboard on chlamydia.

Data
Training line-list data for fictional Springville County, TX, 2012–2015, plus county population denominators
Tools
Excel (merging, formulas, pivot tables, filterable dashboard), Tableau
Role
Sole analyst

My contribution: all of it. The course supplied the raw files and prompts; I wrote the cleaning rules, data dictionary and rate calculations and designed every dashboard.

  1. Merged the disease files into one table with Dx code, sex, race, city, ZIP, age and diagnosis date, keeping the source file name for traceability.
  2. Derived age groups by formula, flagging ages above 120 or missing as invalid, and mapped Dx and race codes to labels with lookup tables.
  3. Removed 310 records: 7 with Dx codes outside the STI codebook and 303 diagnosed outside 2012–2015.
  4. Calculated incidence per 100,000 by year, sex, age group, race/ethnicity and city, then built Tableau dashboards covering person, place and time.

Rates dipped to 463.8 per 100,000 in 2014, then rose to 500.2 in 2015. I also wrote data-entry recommendations for the collection team, including validation rules, dropdowns for coded fields, and consistent patient IDs.

Dietary intake and serum cholesterol in U.S. adults

SAS

Do adults with high cholesterol eat differently? We tested whether carbohydrate, protein and fat intake from a 24-hour dietary recall differ by cholesterol level and whether they predict total cholesterol.

Data
NHANES 2011–2012, adults 18+, N = 4,203
Methods
Wilcoxon rank-sum, chi-square, t-tests, one-way ANOVA with Bartlett and Tukey HSD, simple and multiple linear regression with residual and Q-Q diagnostics
Tools
SAS
Role
Team of three

My contribution: co-wrote the analytic plan, ran the SAS cleaning and analysis, and wrote the final report. Public NHANES data.

IIIAge0.215<0.0001

40.8% of adults had total cholesterol of 200 mg/dL or more. The high group ate less carbohydrate than the normal and borderline groups (Tukey), but diet explained under 1.3% of the variance (R² = 0.0121 for Model III). Age was the strongest predictor.

Long COVID by age group

Infographic · Press release

I analyzed CDC's post-COVID conditions estimates and wrote a plain-language press release and infographic on which age groups report long COVID most often.

Data
U.S. Census Bureau Household Pulse Survey via CDC/NCHS, June 1–13, 2022
Tools
Excel, infographic design
Role
Sole author

My contribution: chose the indicators, extracted the estimates from CDC's public file, and wrote the brief and infographic.

Adults aged 18–29 reported the highest share, 17.8% (95% CI 15.9–19.8), and the share fell steadily after age 60 to 4.2% among adults 80 and older.

Preventive dental visits in Texas children

Data brief

A two-page data brief comparing preventive dental visits for children aged 1–5 and 12–17 locally, statewide and nationally, framed against Healthy People 2030 objective OH-01.

Data
Cook Children's Community Health Needs Assessment 2021 (eight North Texas counties); National Survey of Children's Health 2021
Tools
Excel charts, data brief layout
Role
Sole author

My contribution: pulled comparable estimates from two public sources, calculated the age gaps, built the charts and wrote the brief.

Younger children lagged in every region, and the gap was widest in the Cook Children's service area at 23 points, which points to local barriers to early dental care.

Teen vaping and persistent sadness, 2019–2021

Trend analysis

Using weighted national estimates, I tracked daily e-cigarette use and persistent sadness or hopelessness among U.S. high school students, then compared subgroups by sex, grade and race/ethnicity.

Data
Youth Risk Behavior Survey, 2019 and 2021, grades 9–12
Methods
Weighted prevalence with 95% CIs, comparison across years and subgroups, ecological cross-comparison
Role
Sole author

My contribution: compared CDC's published weighted estimates across years and subgroups and wrote the report. No microdata analysis.

Daily vaping fell from 7.2% to 5.0% while persistent sadness rose from 36.7% to 42.3%, reaching 56.6% among female students in 2021.
